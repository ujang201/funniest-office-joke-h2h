One short line about office life is the whole entry. A judge model reads entries two at a time against a published brief and keeps the funnier one.

## Entry

> I sent a reply-all asking everyone to stop hitting reply-all, and forty-seven people replied-all to agree.

The joke is assembled in `main.ts` from its three comic beats — setup, escalation, punchline last — validated against the gate (1–200 UTF-8 characters, single line, not only whitespace) and written deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
